## Deadline
- Submission opens Saturday Nov 14, 9:00 a.m. and closes **Sunday Nov 15, 11:59 p.m., in the time zone of the team's Local Event** (Universal Event: Team Owner's local time).
- "Any projects saved or submitted past this deadline will not be eligible for judging." Edits and resubmits are allowed until then; only the last saved submission is judged.
- The team is registered at the **Mansoura Local Event** (Tarek, 2026-11-15), so the deadline is 11:59 PM Egypt time (UTC+2) tonight. STILL UNVERIFIED: whether the Mansoura Local Lead sets an earlier local judging or pitch time; ask the Local Lead.
- "Teams are not allowed to begin working on the challenges prior to the hackathon." (FAQ)

## Required fields on the Project page
1. Project Name
2. High-Level Project Summary (what, how it addresses the challenge, why it matters)
3. Link to Project Demo
4. Link to Final Project
5. Detailed Project Description (what it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools and languages)
6. Use of Super Intelligence (SI)
7. NASA Data (specific data and resources used)
8. Space Agency Partner Data and Other References (all data, resources, tools, code, text, images, even if open source)

Two checkboxes at submit: Terms and Conditions, and "Confirm All Work is Original". All required fields must be complete or the Submit button will not work. All content in English. Images can be uploaded to the page (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB); videos and documents cannot.

## Demo
- **Either a video of 30 seconds or less, or a slide deck of at most 7 slides** (including title and intro slides).
- "Projects that exceed these limits will not be eligible for Global Judging."
- Hosted externally with a public link: no password, permission or registration.
- English. A video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles. Slides must be in English.
- Slides must include team name, names of team members, and the challenge selected.
- No image, video, audio or likeness of anyone under 18.
- UNVERIFIED: length and format of any live pitch at the Local Event. The guides do not mention a live pitch.

## Final project link
- Must be publicly accessible, no login. A code repository is acceptable.
- So the GitHub repo (or a hosted site) has to be public by submission time. Decision for G6.
- Optional: FortiGuard web-filter classification for a newly created website.

## AI (SI) disclosure rule and format
- SI tools are permitted. "SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)."
- The project form has a field: "Did you utilize any Super Intelligence (SI) tools and software in preparing your solution? If yes, how did you use them?" The FAQ adds that each submission "must include a description of how SI tools were used".
- "We require that you clearly indicate where and how SI tools were utilized in your project, including":
  - Images and videos: visible watermark.
  - Audio: acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata.
  - **Code and data: "Must acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata."**
- Generated content may not use or modify NASA branding (logos, flags, mission identifiers).
- The form answer is for data collection and is not seen by judges, but judges see the indicators inside the project.
- UNVERIFIED: what exactly counts as "descriptive text and metadata" for code. No example is given.

## Eligibility
- Respond to one official challenge. Use at least one NASA dataset or resource (needed for Global Awards).
- Team of 1 to 6 for a Local Event; every member registered at the same Local Event, not waitlisted, and shown on the team's Members tab. No new members after submission closes.
- China-affiliation review applies to Global Judging (see Terms and Conditions).
- UNVERIFIED: the Participant Terms & Conditions were not opened (licence or IP clauses not checked).

## Judging criteria (each scored 1 to 5, at every level)
| Criterion | What a 5 looks like (summary of the guide) |
|---|---|
| Impact | Addresses a significant problem; wide reach |
| Creativity | Novel approach or a meaningful improvement on existing ideas |
| Validity | Scientifically sound, technically feasible, complete, usable |
| Relevance | Fully addresses the chosen challenge; NASA data is a core component, not a superficial addition |
| Presentation | Clear, well-structured story; judged from the demo and the full project |

"The majority of judges' evaluation will be based on your project submission"; the demo feeds mainly into Presentation.

Dates: Global Nominees Dec 3, 2026; Finalists Dec 17, 2026; Winners Jan 26, 2027. Ten Global Awards, including Best Use of Data, Best Use of Science and Local Impact.
