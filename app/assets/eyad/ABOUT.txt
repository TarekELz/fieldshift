Eyad avatar pictures (eyad_*.jpg)

These pictures were generated with an AI image tool (Google Gemini) from a prompt supplied by the team, and
show a stylized cartoon character. NASA Space Apps rules require AI-generated images to carry a visible
watermark: the app draws an "AI" mark on every avatar and the words "AI-generated" on the large one.
Do not remove the mark. Do not use these pictures anywhere without it (slides, video, README).
The character is a stylized likeness of a person; the team confirms that person's consent and that they are
over 18 (the competition does not allow likenesses of people under 18).
